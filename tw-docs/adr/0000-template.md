# NNNN. Title in the imperative

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who decided

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, constraints, facts from the code. Link notes and diagrams.

## Decision

What we will do, in one or two short paragraphs. Use "we will".

## Options considered

- **Option A** — pros, cons.
- **Option B** — pros, cons.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what follow-up work this creates.
